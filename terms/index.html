What Midstone does

Midstone answers enquiries on your WhatsApp number using the listing facts you approve, offers and books viewings in your calendar, sends reminders and follow-ups you switch on, and shows you every chat so you can step in at any time. When you type in a chat, the assistant goes quiet.

You stay the agent

You remain the licensed agent and are responsible for your listings, your enquiries and your dealings under CEA rules. Midstone is software working under your name and control. It does not negotiate, gives no legal, tax or financing advice, and hands those topics to you. Keep your listing facts accurate; the assistant only says what you have given it.

Honesty about the AI

The assistant does not announce itself as an AI up front, but if anyone asks, it says honestly that it is an AI assistant. It never claims to be human.

Your WhatsApp number

Midstone connects to your number as a linked device. WhatsApp's own terms restrict automated and unofficial connections, so WhatsApp could limit or ban a number. We keep the risk low: replies only to people who message you first, no bulk or cold messages, and follow-ups paced like a person. We cannot guarantee WhatsApp's decisions, and we are not liable for lost business if WhatsApp restricts your number. If it happens, we stop sending at once and give you every lead and chat we hold.
