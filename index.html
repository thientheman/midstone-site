What Midstone does

The repeat work around every enquiry, done.

Answers from your own listings.

Replies on WhatsApp in your tone, using only the facts in your listings. If the listing does not say, it asks you instead of guessing.

Qualifies the lead.

Budget, timing, financing, buying or renting, asked naturally and in order.

Books viewings end to end.

Offers real free times from your calendar, checks access with the occupier, and confirms only when both sides agree. Reminders go out before the viewing.

Follows up by itself.

Day 1, 3, 7 and 14, stopping the moment the lead replies, books or says no.

You stay in control

Your chats. Your clients. Your call.

  • Take over any chat and Midstone goes quiet until you hand it back.
  • Price talk, "can I speak to a real person", legal and tax questions always come to you.
  • Every reply and booking has a short record of what Midstone checked before it acted.
  • One switch pauses it, for one chat or for everything.

Built to be careful

The AI writes. Plain rules check.

Before anything is sent, plain rules check the reply against your listing and your calendar: no price, time, unit number or promise that is not in your own data. If a check fails, the reply is rewritten or handed to you.
