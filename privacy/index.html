Midstone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Midstone is an AI assistant for property agents in Singapore. It answers enquiries on the agent's own WhatsApp number, books viewings into the agent's calendar and follows up with leads. Midstone is being incorporated in Singapore as Midstone AI Pte. Ltd.; until then it is run by its founder, Thien. In this policy, "we" means Midstone, and "you" means the agent or agency using it.

Questions or requests: thien@blueflameautomations.com

What we store

  • Your account: your name, email, agency, CEA registration number and the settings you choose (working hours, viewing length, how the assistant should sound).
  • Your listings: the listing details you give us or the Google Sheet you link, such as price, size, availability, photos, viewing rules and the occupier's or owner's viewing preferences.
  • Your chats: we store the WhatsApp chats on the number your agency connects (group chats only if you choose to include them), plus up to 90 days of earlier history imported into your Midstone inbox, so you can read and reply in Midstone. They are used only for your agency. The AI assistant only works on chats it recognises as property enquiries; it never uses your other chats.
  • Your Google access: when you connect your Google Calendar (we set this up with you during onboarding until the self-serve button is ready), we use Google Calendar only to read your busy and free times and to add the viewings Midstone books for you. A viewing event includes the lead's name, phone number and the property address; a cancelled viewing is marked "[Cancelled]" rather than deleted. We use Google Drive only for files Midstone creates (such as your backups) or files you pick. We do not access Gmail.
  • Leads' details: a lead's name and number as they appear on WhatsApp, and what they tell us in the chat (for example move-in date, number of people, budget), so the assistant can book the right viewing.

How we use it

Only to run Midstone for you: answering your enquiries from your listing facts, offering and booking viewings, sending reminders and follow-ups you have switched on, and showing you your chats, leads and viewings. We use your data for nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Google user data

Midstone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • we use Google Calendar data only to check your availability and to manage the viewing events Midstone books for you, and Google Drive only for files Midstone creates or files you pick; we do not access Gmail;
  • we do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to provide this service, to comply with law, or with your consent;
  • we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it unless you ask us to (for support), it is needed for security, or the law requires it;
  • we do not use Google user data to train AI models.

Disconnect any time: remove Midstone from your Google Account (Security, Third-party apps with account access), or press Disconnect in Midstone, or ask us. We then delete the stored tokens.

Who else handles it

  • Hosting: your data is stored on our server in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur), run by our hosting provider, and kept separate per agency so one agency can never see another's. Google access tokens are kept in restricted-access files on that server.
  • AI processing: to write a reply, the AI model provider we use receives the recent conversation (up to the last 20 messages), the lead's first name and the details they gave, and the listing facts. If you use Midstone's phone answering, call audio is processed by a real-time voice AI provider. Voice notes are transcribed on our own server. We do not use your data to train AI models, and we configure the AI services we use not to train on it.
  • WhatsApp: messages travel through WhatsApp. Midstone connects to your number as a linked device, the same way WhatsApp Web does.

Our server and our AI providers are outside Singapore. We require them to protect your data to a standard comparable to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

Your data stays yours

Your listings, chats and leads belong to you. Ask us and we export them for you. We never contact your leads for anyone but you, and we never share them with other agents or agencies.

Keeping and deleting data

  • We keep your data while you use Midstone.
  • Ask us to delete it at any time (thien@blueflameautomations.com), or stop using Midstone, and we delete your account data, listings, chats and leads within 30 days, and confirm in writing.
  • Backups are deleted within 30 days.

Leads who message an agent

If you messaged a property agent who uses Midstone, the agent decides how your details are used, and the assistant works on their behalf. The assistant will always say honestly that it is an AI assistant if you ask. To have your messages deleted, ask the agent, or email us and we will pass it on and act on the agent's instruction.

Security

Access is limited to the people who need it to run the service. Connections are encrypted, and each agency's data is kept separate. If we ever find a breach that affects your data, we will tell you without delay.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw a consent you gave, by emailing thien@blueflameautomations.com. We reply within 7 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or WhatsApp before it applies. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
